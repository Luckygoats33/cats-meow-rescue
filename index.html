What We Do & Why

Every Cat Gets a Shot at a Better Life

Rescue

Foster

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about adopting, fostering, and supporting our rescue cats.

Browse our adoptable cats on this page or on Petfinder. When you find a cat you love, fill out our adoption application above. We'll review it, schedule a meet-and-greet, and if it's a good match, your new best friend comes home!
Adoption fees vary by age: kittens (under 1 year) are $150, adult cats (1-7 years) are $100, and senior cats (7+ years) are $75. All fees go directly back into rescue operations, medical care, and food for our foster cats.
Every adopted cat comes spayed or neutered, up-to-date on vaccinations (FVRCP and rabies), microchipped, dewormed, flea-treated, and FIV/FeLV tested. You also receive a starter kit with food samples and a health record.
Absolutely! Once your application is reviewed, we'll arrange a meet-and-greet at the foster home or one of our PetSmart adoption events in Milwaukie and Gladstone. We want to make sure it's the perfect match for both you and the cat.
We frequently have kittens, especially during spring and summer (kitten season). Check our adoptable cats section for current availability. Kittens go fast, so if you see one you love, apply quickly!
We're always looking for foster homes! Fill out our application above and select "Foster" as the type. We provide all food, litter, and medical care — you provide the love and a safe space. Fostering saves lives by freeing up space for more rescues.
Click the "Donate" button on this page to make a one-time or recurring gift. We also accept donations of cat food, litter, blankets, and toys. As a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, all donations are tax-deductible. Every dollar helps save a cat's life.
Unlike traditional shelters, we don't have a physical facility. All our cats live in loving foster homes until they're adopted. This means less stress for the cats and more one-on-one attention, which helps us learn each cat's personality to find them the best forever home.
Yes! We need help with adoption events at PetSmart, transporting cats to vet appointments, socializing kittens, photography, social media, fundraising, and more. Fill out our volunteer application to get started.
Cat's Meow Cat Rescue is based in Milwaukie and Gladstone, Oregon. As a foster-based rescue, we don't have a storefront, but you can meet our cats at PetSmart adoption events or at foster homes by appointment. Our mailing address is PO Box 68222, Milwaukie, OR 97268.
