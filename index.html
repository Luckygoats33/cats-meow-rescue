What We Do & Why

Every Cat Gets a Shot at a Better Life

Rescue

We take in abandoned, surrendered, and stray cats from the Portland metro area and give them immediate care.

Foster

Every cat lives in a real home with a dedicated foster family — never in a cage. We provide all supplies and vet care.

TNR

Our Trap-Neuter-Return program humanely manages feral cat colonies through spay/neuter with FCCO.

Adopt

We carefully match each cat to their perfect family — not first-come, first-served. It's about the right fit.
