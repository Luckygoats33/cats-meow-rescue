Barn Cats

Some cats do better as working cats on a property — barn cats, or cats living with a colony — than as indoor house pets. This page is for people whose property can offer that kind of placement.

Tell us about the space: whether it is a barn, outbuilding, or another property setup, and whether other cats already live there. We will reply by email.

We do not list fees, how many cats we have, or which placements are open on this page.
