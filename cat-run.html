How to play

  • Run: Arrow keys or A and D. On a phone, use Left and Right.
  • Jump: Space, W, or Up, or the Jump button. Hold it for a higher leap. A tap is a short hop.
  • Yarn is 10 points. Fish treats are 25. The porch at the end of a yard is 100.
  • Dogs and vacuums: hop on their heads (50 points) or jump over them. A bump from the side costs a life. So does the creek.
  • Lives: you start with 3. Cat beds remember a safe spot. Reach the porch — Backyard Yarn, then Foster Porch.
  • Restart (or the R key) sends Miso back to the first yard. Sound is a few soft beeps, and you can turn it off.

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue is a foster-based 501(c)(3) in Milwaukie and the Portland metro. This game is only for fun — it does not take applications or gifts. When you want the real thing: apply to adopt, see how to adopt, foster, or donate.
