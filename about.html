About Us

A small group of dedicated individuals saving cats in Milwaukie, Gladstone, and the greater Portland area.

Our Story

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue was born from a simple belief: every cat deserves a loving home. What started as a handful of passionate volunteers rescuing strays from the streets of Milwaukie and Gladstone, Oregon has grown into a dedicated foster-based rescue serving the greater Portland metro area.

We are registered with the State of Oregon and have our 501(c)(3) designation, which means we will give a receipt for any donation of money or supplies for a deduction on your taxes. We have bank and PayPal/Venmo accounts which are used solely to fulfill our mission of helping cats live better lives.

As a proud PetSmart Charities partner, we host regular adoption events at local PetSmart locations, giving our cats maximum visibility and connecting them with potential adopters in a comfortable, accessible setting.

Our Mission

Our mission is to help improve the overall lives of cats in our community and our rescue partners to help find homes for cats. We do this in many ways from trap-neuter-return (TNR) to finding placement for abandoned or unwanted cats. We promise to behave ethically, honestly, and always with the best interests of our community in mind.

We would love to gain your support and look forward to hearing from you. Please reach out to us if you need help with cats.

Rescue

We take in cats from shelters, streets, and surrenders across the Portland metro area.

Rehabilitate

Full vet care, socialization, and foster home recovery until they're ready for adoption.

Rehome

Thoughtful matching ensures every cat finds the right forever family through our adoption process.

How You Can Help

As an all-volunteer organization, we rely entirely on the generosity of our community. Whether you adopt, foster, donate, or volunteer your time, every contribution makes a direct impact on the cats in our care.

Foster homes are always needed — even a few weeks of your time can save a life. Donations fund veterinary care, food, and supplies. And volunteers help at adoption events, transport cats, and spread the word.
