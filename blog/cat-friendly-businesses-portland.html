Cat-Friendly Businesses in the Portland Metro Area

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue · July 5, 2026

Pet Supply Stores

Portland has excellent independent pet supply stores that go above and beyond for cat owners. Mud Bay offers quality foods and knowledgeable staff who can help with nutrition questions. Healthy Pet on SE Hawthorne specializes in natural and holistic pet foods. Pet Valu and many neighborhood shops support local rescues through donation bins and adoption event hosting.

Veterinary Clinics

The Portland area has several cat-specific or cat-friendly veterinary practices. The Cat Hospital of Portland focuses exclusively on feline care. Many general practices, including Milwaukie Veterinary Hospital and Gladstone Animal Hospital, are certified Cat Friendly Practices by the American Association of Feline Practitioners, meaning they follow specific protocols to reduce stress for feline patients.

Cat-Themed Shops and Services

Portland's love for cats extends to retail and services. Cat-themed gift shops, custom cat portrait artists, cat furniture makers, and professional cat sitters are all thriving in the Portland metro area. Many of these businesses donate a portion of their proceeds to local cat rescues, creating a cycle of community support.

Supporting Rescue-Friendly Businesses

When you patronize businesses that support cat rescue, you are indirectly funding life-saving work. Ask your favorite local businesses if they would be willing to host a donation jar for Cat's Meow Cat Rescue, sponsor an adoption event, or feature our available cats in their storefront. Portland's business community is remarkably generous when asked.
