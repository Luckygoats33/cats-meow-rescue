5 Cat Adoption Myths -- Debunked

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue · February 20, 2025

Misconceptions about rescue cats prevent thousands of wonderful animals from finding homes every year. At Cat's Meow Cat Rescue, we hear these myths regularly from well-meaning people who simply have not had the chance to learn the truth. Let us set the record straight on five of the most persistent cat adoption myths.

Myth 1: Shelter Cats Are "Damaged" or Have Behavioral Problems

The truth: The vast majority of cats in rescue are there because of human circumstances, not because anything is wrong with the cat. Owners move, develop allergies, pass away, or face financial hardship. Many of our cats at Cat's Meow came from loving homes and are perfectly socialized, litter-trained, and affectionate.

Cats with known behavioral quirks are always disclosed upfront. Our foster families spend weeks observing each cat's behavior so there are no surprises. In fact, a rescue cat with a documented history often comes with more information than a kitten from an unknown background.

Myth 2: You Cannot Find Kittens or Specific Breeds at a Rescue

The truth: Cat rescues in Oregon regularly have kittens, especially during "kitten season" from spring through fall. Cat's Meow frequently takes in litters of kittens from community cats. As for breeds, we regularly see Siamese mixes, Maine Coon mixes, tuxedos, calicos, and every color and pattern imaginable. If you have a preference, let our team know and we can watch for incoming cats that match what you are looking for.

Myth 3: Adopted Cats Will Never Bond with You Like a Kitten Would

The truth: This is perhaps the most damaging myth of all. Adult and senior cats form deep, lasting bonds with their adopters. Many adopters report that their rescue cat seems to understand they have been given a second chance, showing a level of gratitude and attachment that is genuinely moving. Age has nothing to do with a cat's ability to love you.

Kittens are adorable, but they are also unpredictable. Their adult personality has not yet developed. With an adult rescue cat, you know exactly what kind of companion you are getting.

Myth 4: Rescue Cats Are Unhealthy

The truth: Reputable rescues like Cat's Meow ensure every cat receives comprehensive veterinary care before adoption. Our cats are:

  • Spayed or neutered
  • Up to date on vaccinations
  • Tested for common diseases like FIV and FeLV
  • Microchipped
  • Treated for any existing medical issues

In many cases, an adopted cat arrives with better documented medical history than a kitten purchased from other sources. And our adoption fees cover hundreds of dollars worth of veterinary care that you would otherwise pay out of pocket.

Myth 5: The Adoption Process Is Too Difficult or Invasive

The truth: Our adoption process at Cat's Meow is designed to be thorough but not burdensome. We ask questions about your home, lifestyle, and experience with cats because we want to make a match that works for everyone. The application, meet-and-greet, and approval process can often be completed within a few days.

We are not trying to find reasons to say no. We are trying to help you find the right cat. Our adoption counselors are on your side, guiding you toward a match that will bring years of happiness. Visit us at our PetSmart partner location in the Portland metro area, or check our available cats online to see who is waiting for you.
