Traveling with Your Cat in Oregon

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue · July 7, 2026

Road Trip Preparation

Before a road trip, acclimate your cat to the carrier and short car rides. Pack essentials: carrier, food, water, portable litter box, litter, medications, vaccination records, and a recent photo in case your cat escapes. Feed a small meal 3-4 hours before departure to reduce car sickness. Never let your cat roam free in the car while driving.

Finding Cat-Friendly Accommodations

Many Oregon hotels and vacation rentals accept cats, though policies vary. Websites like BringFido.com and Airbnb allow you to filter for pet-friendly options. Popular Oregon coast destinations like Cannon Beach, Lincoln City, and Newport have numerous cat-friendly lodging options. Always confirm the pet policy and any additional fees before booking.

Alternatives to Traveling with Your Cat

Most cats are happiest in their own environment. For trips longer than a day, consider hiring a professional cat sitter who visits your home once or twice daily. Portland has many reputable pet sitting services. Alternatively, boarding facilities like luxury cat hotels offer individualized suites and webcam monitoring. Cat's Meow Cat Rescue can recommend trusted pet sitters in the Portland area.

Emergency Preparedness

When traveling with your cat in Oregon, carry your veterinarian's contact information and the number for the nearest emergency vet clinic at your destination. Ensure your cat's microchip registration is current. Keep your cat's carrier secured in the car with a seatbelt. Oregon's scenic beauty and outdoor opportunities make it a wonderful state for adventures, and with proper preparation, your cat can safely be part of them.
