Cat Cafes in Portland: Where Coffee Meets Rescue Cats

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue · July 14, 2026

Portland's Cat Cafe Scene

Portland has embraced the cat cafe concept with characteristic enthusiasm. These establishments combine a coffee shop with an adjoining room full of adoptable cats from local rescues. Visitors pay a small fee to spend time with the cats, and many cafes have facilitated thousands of adoptions since opening. It is a uniquely Portland way to match cats with potential adopters.

How Cat Cafes Partner with Rescues

Cat cafes work with local rescues to provide a storefront for adoptable cats. The cats live at the cafe during business hours and return to foster homes at night. This model gives rescue cats much more visibility than online listings alone. For rescues like Cat's Meow Cat Rescue, cat cafe partnerships can significantly increase adoption rates.

Visiting a Cat Cafe

Most Portland cat cafes require a reservation for the cat room, especially on weekends. Expect to pay $10-$15 for a 30-60 minute session. Cafes typically serve coffee, tea, and light snacks in a separate area. Sanitize your hands before entering the cat room, follow the cafe's rules about handling, and enjoy the experience of spending time with cats in a relaxed, comfortable setting.

Beyond Entertainment

Cat cafes serve important purposes beyond entertainment. They provide socialization for cats waiting for adoption, raise awareness about rescue cats, generate revenue that supports rescue operations, and offer therapeutic benefits to visitors. Studies show that spending time with cats reduces cortisol levels and increases oxytocin, making cat cafes genuine stress-relief destinations.
