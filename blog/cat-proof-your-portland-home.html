Cat-Proofing Your Portland Home: Room by Room

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue · July 14, 2026

Kitchen

Secure cabinet doors that contain cleaning products. Remove or secure trash cans with lids. Keep counters clear of sharp objects, toxic foods (onions, garlic, chocolate), and small items that could be swallowed. Ensure stovetop knobs cannot be accidentally activated. Keep the dishwasher door closed, as cats can climb inside. Secure any cords from small appliances.

Living Areas

Secure or hide electrical cords behind furniture or in cord covers. Remove or secure blinds cords, which are strangulation hazards. Move poisonous houseplants out of reach (lilies are especially dangerous). Secure heavy furniture and TVs that could be toppled by a climbing cat. Check that window screens are secure if you plan to open windows.

Bathroom

Keep the toilet lid closed to prevent drowning risk for kittens. Store medications in closed cabinets. Remove toxic cleaning products from accessible areas. Secure trash cans. Be cautious with essential oil diffusers, which are toxic to cats. Keep dental floss and hair ties out of reach, as they are common causes of intestinal blockages.

Bedroom and Laundry

Check inside the dryer before every use, as cats love to climb into warm laundry. Keep small items like jewelry, hair ties, and sewing supplies off surfaces. Secure window screens. Be mindful of closet doors, as cats can get trapped inside. In Portland's rainy season, be aware that damp shoes may attract cats who like to chew on laces.
