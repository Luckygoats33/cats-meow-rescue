Fostering Cats During Portland's Summer: Special Considerations

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue · June 29, 2026

Heat Management

Portland summers have become increasingly intense, with heat domes and multi-day stretches above 100 degrees. Foster cats, especially kittens and seniors, are vulnerable to heat stress. Ensure your foster room has air conditioning or good ventilation. Provide multiple water sources, frozen water bottles wrapped in towels for cooling, and tile or marble surfaces for the cat to lie on.

Kitten Season Overlap

Summer is the peak of kitten season in Oregon. Cat's Meow Cat Rescue receives the most kittens from May through September. Summer fosters are critically needed for the flood of kittens entering our system. If you can only foster once a year, summer is when your help matters most.

Wildfire Smoke

Portland's wildfire smoke season typically peaks in August and September. Keep foster cats indoors with windows closed during smoke events. Run air purifiers if available. Cats with respiratory conditions are especially vulnerable. Monitor air quality at airnow.gov and contact Cat's Meow Cat Rescue if your foster cat shows signs of respiratory distress during smoke events.
