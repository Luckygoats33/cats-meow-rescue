Advocating for Cat-Friendly Housing Policies in Oregon

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue · July 1, 2026

The Housing Problem for Pet Owners

Finding pet-friendly housing is one of the biggest challenges for cat owners in the Portland metro area. Rising rents and limited availability mean pet owners often have fewer housing options, pay higher deposits, or face outright pet bans. This housing pressure is a leading reason cats are surrendered to rescues.

What Advocates Are Doing

Oregon advocacy groups are working to expand pet-friendly housing access. Efforts include pushing for legislation limiting pet deposits, opposing blanket pet bans in rental housing, and creating incentive programs for landlords who accept pets. The Oregon Humane Society and other organizations lobby at the state and local level for pet-friendly housing policies.

How You Can Help

Contact your state representative to support pet-friendly housing legislation. Write positive reviews for pet-friendly landlords and property management companies. Support organizations like Cat's Meow Cat Rescue that help keep cats in homes through financial assistance and pet food programs. Every voice matters in the push for more inclusive housing policies.
