Fostering & Foster Care

Foster FAQ: Everything Portland Cat Lovers Want to Know

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue · July 10, 2026

Do I Need Experience?

No. Cat's Meow Cat Rescue provides thorough orientation, ongoing training, and 24/7 support for all foster parents. Whether you have never had a cat or have decades of experience, we will match you with a foster cat appropriate for your skill level. First-time fosters often start with friendly adult cats or supervised kitten litters.

What If I Get Attached?

This is the number one concern new fosters express. Many foster parents do get attached, and some become "foster failures" who end up adopting their foster cat. We celebrate these outcomes because it means the cat found a perfect home. However, most fosters learn that saying goodbye means opening their home for the next cat in need, and the joy of saving multiple lives outweighs the sadness of letting go.

How Long Will I Have the Cat?

It varies. Healthy, friendly adult cats may be adopted within 2-4 weeks. Kittens stay until they are 8-12 weeks old and reach adoption weight (2 pounds). Shy or special needs cats may need longer. You can set a timeframe with our team and we will work within your availability.

Can I Foster If I Rent?

In most cases, yes. Since Cat's Meow Cat Rescue retains ownership of the foster cat, many landlords are more flexible about fostering than permanent pet ownership. We can provide documentation that the cat is a temporary foster under our organization's care. Always check with your landlord first, and we are happy to help with that conversation.
