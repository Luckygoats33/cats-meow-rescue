Cat Rescue Organizations in the Portland Metro Area

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue · April 5, 2025

The State of Cat Rescue in Oregon

Oregon has a thriving network of cat rescue organizations, and the Portland metro area is home to some of the most active and dedicated groups in the Pacific Northwest. Each year, thousands of cats and kittens enter shelters and rescue programs across Clackamas, Multnomah, and Washington counties. The collaborative efforts of these organizations save lives every day.

Whether you are looking to adopt, volunteer, foster, or donate, understanding the landscape of local rescues helps you find the right organization to support or work with.

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue: Milwaukie and Gladstone

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, foster-based rescue operating in Milwaukie and Gladstone, Oregon. Our all-volunteer team focuses on rescuing cats from high-risk situations, providing full veterinary care, and placing them in loving permanent homes through our PetSmart adoption events and direct adoption process.

As a foster-based organization, we do not operate a brick-and-mortar shelter. Instead, every cat in our care lives in a volunteer foster home where they receive individualized attention and socialization until they are adopted. This model allows us to take in cats that might otherwise be overlooked in crowded shelter environments.

Other Portland Metro Area Cat Rescues

The Portland area benefits from several outstanding organizations focused on cat welfare:

  • Cat Adoption Team (CAT): Located in Sherwood, CAT is one of the largest cat-only shelters on the West Coast, rescuing over 3,000 cats annually. They offer adoption, foster, and TNR programs.
  • Oregon Humane Society: Portland's oldest and largest animal welfare organization handles cats, dogs, and other animals. Their Northeast Portland campus includes a full veterinary hospital.
  • Furry Friends: A no-kill cat rescue in Vancouver, Washington, just across the Columbia River, focusing on rescuing cats from high-kill shelters.
  • Animal Aid: Based in Southwest Portland, Animal Aid is a no-kill shelter that serves cats and dogs with a focus on senior and special-needs animals.
  • Multnomah County Animal Services: The county shelter in Troutdale handles stray and surrendered animals, and works closely with rescue organizations to place cats in homes.

How Rescues Work Together

One of the strengths of the Portland rescue community is how organizations collaborate. County shelters transfer cats to foster-based rescues like Cat's Meow when their facilities reach capacity. Rescues share resources, coordinate transport, and refer adopters to each other when a particular cat might be a better fit elsewhere.

This network approach means that no single organization has to carry the burden alone. It also means that adopters have more options and a better chance of finding the right cat for their family.

How to Connect with a Rescue Near You

If you are in the Portland metro area and want to help or adopt, here are some practical steps:

  1. Search Petfinder or Adopt-a-Pet to see available cats from multiple rescues in one place.
  2. Attend local adoption events at PetSmart, Petco, and independent pet stores to meet cats and talk with rescue volunteers.
  3. Follow local rescues on social media for urgent needs, new arrivals, and event announcements.
  4. Contact your nearest rescue directly to ask about fostering, volunteering, or donating supplies.

Every rescue in the Portland area shares the same goal: giving every cat the chance at a safe and happy life. Whether you choose to support Cat's Meow Cat Rescue or another organization, your involvement makes a measurable difference.
