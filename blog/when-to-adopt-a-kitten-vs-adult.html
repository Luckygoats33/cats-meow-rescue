Kitten vs. Adult Cat: Which Should You Adopt?

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue · July 8, 2026

The Appeal of Kittens

Kittens are undeniably adorable, and many people default to wanting a kitten when they decide to adopt. Kittens are playful, curious, and fun to watch as they explore the world. However, kittens also require significantly more work than adult cats. They need to be fed more frequently, litter trained, socialized, and kitten-proofed away from household hazards.

The Case for Adult Cats

Adult cats are the hidden gems of the rescue world. Their personalities are fully developed, so you know exactly what you are getting. They are typically already litter trained, less destructive, and more likely to be calm and affectionate. Adult cats at Cat's Meow Cat Rescue have been thoroughly assessed by foster parents who can tell you every detail about their behavior and preferences.

Lifestyle Considerations

If you work full-time, live in a small apartment, or prefer a calm household, an adult cat is almost certainly the better choice. If you have young children who want an active playmate and you have the time to supervise and train, a kitten might work. Regardless of your choice, Cat's Meow Cat Rescue can help match you with the right cat for your Portland-area home.

The Bottom Line

Both kittens and adult cats make wonderful companions. The best choice depends on your lifestyle, experience, and what you are looking for in a pet. Talk to our adoption team, and we will help you find the perfect match.
