Eco-Friendly Cat Care: Sustainable Choices for Cat Owners

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue · Milwaukie, Oregon

Cat ownership has an environmental footprint — from litter waste to food production to toy consumption. Here's how to make more sustainable choices without compromising your cat's care.

Sustainable Litter

Traditional clay litter is strip-mined and doesn't biodegrade. Eco-friendly alternatives include: wood pellet litter (made from sawmill byproducts), paper-based litter (recycled newspaper), corn or wheat litter (biodegradable and often compostable), and grass seed litter (lightweight and sustainable). Test a few to find one your cat accepts — transition gradually over 1-2 weeks.

Responsible Food Choices

Cats are obligate carnivores, so plant-based diets aren't appropriate. But you can choose: brands with sustainable sourcing practices, foods with responsibly caught fish (look for MSC certification), and brands that use recyclable or minimal packaging. Buy in bulk to reduce packaging waste.

DIY and Upcycled Toys

Skip the plastic toy aisle and make your own: cardboard boxes, paper bags (handles removed), crinkled paper balls, old t-shirt braids, and sock catnip toys. See our enrichment guide for more ideas. When buying toys, choose natural materials (wool, sisal, organic cotton) over plastic.

Reduce, Reuse, Recycle

Donate unused pet supplies to Cat's Meow or other local rescues rather than throwing them away. Use washable food and water bowls (ceramic or stainless steel, not disposable). Choose reusable litter liners instead of plastic bags. Compost used plant-based litter (not in food gardens — cat waste can contain pathogens).

The Biggest Impact: Keep Cats Indoors

The single most impactful environmental choice for cat owners is keeping cats indoors. Indoor cats don't hunt wildlife, reducing the estimated 1.3-4 billion birds killed annually by outdoor cats in the U.S. This is especially important in Oregon's biodiverse Pacific Northwest ecosystem.

For more on sustainable pet ownership, the One Green Planet has resources on eco-friendly animal care.
