Eco-Friendly Cat Care: Sustainable Choices for Cat Owners

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue · July 8, 2025

As cat lovers, we want the best for our feline companions and for the planet they share with us. The pet industry generates significant waste, from plastic packaging to clay litter mined from the earth. But with a few thoughtful changes, you can reduce your environmental impact without compromising your cat's health or happiness. Here are practical ways to make your cat care routine more sustainable.

Choosing Eco-Friendly Litter

Traditional clay cat litter is strip-mined and not biodegradable, contributing to landfill waste. Fortunately, greener alternatives abound:

  • Wood pellet litter: Made from reclaimed sawdust, it is highly absorbent, naturally controls odor, and can be composted (for non-edible gardens only).
  • Walnut shell litter: A byproduct of walnut processing that clumps well and is biodegradable.
  • Recycled paper litter: Low-dust and lightweight, made from post-consumer recycled paper.
  • Corn and wheat litters: Renewable, clumping, and flushable in many cases (check your local water treatment guidelines first).
  • Grass seed litter: Lightweight, clumping, and compostable.

Transition tip: Cats can be particular about litter changes. Mix the new litter with your current brand gradually over two weeks, increasing the ratio until the switch is complete.

Sustainable Food Choices

Cat food is the biggest contributor to a cat's environmental pawprint. While cats are obligate carnivores and need meat-based protein, you can still make more sustainable choices:

  • Choose brands that use sustainably sourced proteins and transparent supply chains.
  • Look for packaging that is recyclable or made from recycled materials. Some brands now offer compostable packaging.
  • Buy in bulk when possible to reduce packaging waste per serving.
  • Avoid overfeeding. Portion control reduces waste and keeps your cat at a healthy weight.
  • Consider brands that use byproduct proteins (organ meats and parts humans typically discard). Despite marketing stigma, these are nutritious for cats and reduce food system waste.

Reducing Plastic and Waste

Small changes in your daily routine add up over time:

  • Replace plastic food and water bowls with stainless steel or ceramic options. They last longer, harbor less bacteria, and are fully recyclable.
  • Use biodegradable waste bags for litter box scooping instead of plastic grocery bags.
  • Choose toys made from natural materials like wool, hemp, cotton, or sustainably harvested wood. Many locally made cat toys at Oregon craft markets use recycled or organic materials.
  • Repurpose old t-shirts, socks, and cardboard boxes as DIY cat toys and beds rather than buying new.
  • Donate gently used cat supplies (carriers, beds, scratching posts) to local rescues like Cat's Meow rather than throwing them away.

Eco-Conscious Veterinary Care

Preventive care is the most sustainable approach to your cat's health. Keeping your cat healthy reduces the resources consumed by emergency treatments, medications, and specialty procedures:

  • Stay current on vaccinations and wellness exams to catch problems early.
  • Spay or neuter your cat. Preventing unwanted litters is the single most impactful environmental choice a cat owner can make. Every cat adopted from Cat's Meow Cat Rescue is spayed or neutered before going home.
  • Ask your vet about refillable medication containers and proper disposal of unused medications through pharmacy take-back programs.

Supporting Sustainable Rescue Work

Adopting from a rescue like Cat's Meow Cat Rescue is itself an eco-friendly choice. Rescue organizations reduce the demand for commercially bred animals and give existing cats a second chance. You can further support sustainable rescue work by:

  • Donating clean, used towels and blankets instead of buying new ones for shelters.
  • Volunteering your time for foster care, transport, or adoption events at our PetSmart locations.
  • Supporting trap-neuter-return (TNR) programs that humanely manage community cat populations.

Every sustainable choice you make, no matter how small, helps create a better world for cats and the communities we share. At Cat's Meow Cat Rescue in Milwaukie, Oregon, we are committed to modeling eco-friendly practices in our own operations and helping our adopters do the same.
