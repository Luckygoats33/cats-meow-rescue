10 Questions to Ask Before Adopting a Cat

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue · July 7, 2026

Questions to Ask Yourself

Before contacting a rescue, ask yourself: Can I commit to 15-20 years of pet ownership? Can I afford veterinary care, food, and supplies ($500-$1,000 per year)? Does my living situation allow cats? Do all household members agree? Am I prepared for the adjustment period? Being honest about these questions prevents returned adoptions, which are stressful for both cats and families.

Questions to Ask the Rescue

When speaking with Cat's Meow Cat Rescue or any other organization, ask about the cat's medical history, behavioral temperament, how they interact with other animals and children, what food they are currently eating, and what their daily routine looks like. Our foster parents are happy to answer all of these questions because we want successful, permanent placements.

Questions About the Adoption Process

Ask about the timeline from application to adoption, the adoption fee and what it includes, the return policy if things do not work out, and what post-adoption support is available. At Cat's Meow, we have a straightforward process and offer ongoing support to all adopters.

Questions About Your Specific Situation

If you have specific concerns, such as allergies, travel schedules, or existing pets, ask the rescue for guidance. We have helped adopters in every situation imaginable, and we would rather address potential issues upfront than have an adoption fail later.
