Why 'Adopt Don't Shop' Matters More Than Ever

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue · Milwaukie, Oregon

The "adopt don't shop" movement isn't about shaming anyone — it's about making informed choices that save lives. Here's why rescue adoption matters.

The Numbers

Approximately 3.2 million cats enter U.S. shelters every year. Despite the efforts of rescue organizations like Cat's Meow, about 530,000 cats are euthanized annually — not because they're unhealthy or unadoptable, but simply because there aren't enough homes. Every cat adopted from a rescue directly reduces this number.

What Buying Supports

When you buy a cat from a pet store or online seller, you're often supporting kitten mills — large-scale breeding operations where cats live in cramped, unsanitary conditions and are bred repeatedly for profit. Even "reputable" breeders contribute to the overpopulation problem when millions of cats in rescues need homes.

What Adoption Supports

Your adoption fee funds rescue operations: veterinary care, food, shelter, and the infrastructure that saves more cats. At Cat's Meow, every dollar goes back into our mission. Adoption also frees foster space, allowing us to pull more cats from overcrowded shelters and dangerous situations.

Rescue Cats Are Wonderful Pets

The myths about rescue cats — that they're damaged, sickly, or poorly socialized — are simply not true. Our cats come fully vetted, often have known personality profiles from foster care, and are ready for their forever homes. Many adopters tell us their rescue cat is the best pet they've ever had.

Making the Choice

We understand the desire for specific breeds. But before buying, check breed-specific rescues — they exist for nearly every breed. And consider that a cat's love isn't determined by their pedigree. Browse our available cats and see for yourself.

For more on the adopt-don't-shop movement, the ASPCA provides comprehensive advocacy resources.
