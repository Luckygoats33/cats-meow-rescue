Emergency Fostering: When Cats Need Help Right Now

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue · July 9, 2026

What Is Emergency Fostering?

Emergency fostering is a short-term commitment (typically 1-14 days) to house a cat that needs immediate placement. This happens when cats are pulled from emergency situations, such as hoarding cases, house fires, domestic violence situations, or owner hospitalizations. The cat needs a safe place to stay while Cat's Meow Cat Rescue arranges long-term placement.

Why Emergency Fosters Are Essential

Without emergency foster homes, rescues must turn away cats in crisis. A network of people willing to take a cat on short notice, even for just a few days, dramatically increases our capacity to respond to emergencies. In Portland, where housing instability and natural disasters can displace pets without warning, this capacity saves lives.

What You Need

An emergency foster needs very little advance preparation: a bathroom or spare room that can be closed off, a litter box, and food and water dishes. Cat's Meow Cat Rescue will rush supplies to you and handle all veterinary needs. Your job is simply to provide a calm, safe space for a cat during a frightening transition.

How to Sign Up

If you are interested in being on our emergency foster list, contact Cat's Meow Cat Rescue. We will ask about your availability, home setup, and any limitations (no dogs, no small children, etc.). When an emergency arises, we will call or text to see if you can help. You can always say no if the timing does not work.
