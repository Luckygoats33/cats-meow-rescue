Creative Fundraising Ideas for Animal Rescues

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue · Milwaukie, Oregon

Running a cat rescue costs money — veterinary care alone can run thousands per month. Whether you're organizing a fundraiser for Cat's Meow or another rescue, here are ideas that actually work.

Online Fundraisers

Facebook fundraisers are the easiest to set up and have zero overhead — Facebook covers all processing fees for registered nonprofits. Birthday fundraisers (asking friends to donate instead of giving gifts) consistently perform well. GoFundMe campaigns work best for specific, urgent needs (emergency surgery for a specific cat, for example).

Community Events

Cat yoga, cat trivia nights, and "meow mixers" (cat-themed happy hours) combine fun with fundraising. Partner with local businesses — many Portland-area breweries, cafes, and restaurants will host events and donate a percentage of sales. Silent auctions with donated items are another reliable revenue source.

Ongoing Revenue

Our merch shop generates consistent revenue. Amazon Smile donates 0.5% of eligible purchases. Employer matching programs can double individual donations — encourage donors to check if their company offers matching.

Corporate Partnerships

Local businesses are often willing to sponsor events, donate supplies, or match customer donations. Pet stores, veterinary clinics, and pet-friendly businesses are natural partners. Approach them with a clear proposal showing the partnership's mutual benefits.

Recurring Donations

A $10/month recurring donor is more valuable than a one-time $100 gift because of predictability. Promote monthly giving programs with tangible impact statements: "$10/month feeds a foster cat," "$25/month covers a cat's monthly medical care."

For nonprofit fundraising best practices, Network for Good provides free resources and tools. To donate to Cat's Meow, visit our website.
