Foster-to-Adopt: How Fostering Leads to Forever Homes

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue · March 5, 2025

Adopting a cat is a big decision, and it is completely normal to wonder whether a particular cat will be the right fit for your household. That is exactly why Cat's Meow Cat Rescue offers a foster-to-adopt program — a low-pressure way to welcome a cat into your home, get to know their personality, and decide whether to make the commitment permanent. It is one of the best paths to a successful, lasting adoption.

How Foster-to-Adopt Works

The concept is simple: you foster a cat with the option to adopt them if the match is right. During the foster period, you provide a temporary home while the cat settles in and shows you who they really are. Shelter and adoption-event environments are stressful for cats, and many animals behave very differently once they are in a calm home setting.

At Cat's Meow, the foster-to-adopt period typically lasts two to four weeks. During that time, the rescue continues to cover veterinary expenses and provides food, litter, and support just as we would for any foster placement. If you decide the cat is a perfect match, you complete the adoption paperwork and pay the standard adoption fee. If the match is not quite right, the cat returns to foster status with no guilt or judgment.

Why It Works So Well

Foster-to-adopt addresses one of the biggest challenges in cat adoption: personality mismatches. A cat who seems shy at a PetSmart adoption event might turn out to be a playful lap cat at home. Conversely, a cat described as laid-back might have more energy than your household can accommodate. The trial period eliminates guesswork.

Benefits of foster-to-adopt include:

  • Time to see how the cat interacts with your family, other pets, and daily routine
  • Reduced return rates — cats adopted through foster-to-adopt are far less likely to be returned
  • A smoother transition for the cat, who is already settled into your home by adoption day
  • Confidence in your decision, which makes for a happier adopter and a happier cat

Who Is a Good Candidate?

Foster-to-adopt is especially well suited for:

  • First-time cat owners who want to make sure they are ready for the responsibility
  • Families with other pets who need to test compatibility before committing
  • People considering a shy or special-needs cat who may take longer to show their true personality
  • Anyone who has had a past adoption that did not work out and wants a more cautious approach this time

You do not need previous fostering experience. Our foster coordinators will walk you through setup, introduce you to the cat, and check in regularly throughout the trial period.

What Happens If You Decide Not to Adopt?

There is absolutely no obligation to adopt. If the match is not right for any reason — the cat does not get along with your resident pet, the energy level is not what you expected, or it simply is not the connection you were hoping for — you let your coordinator know and we transition the cat back into our foster network. Your honest feedback actually helps us make a better match for that cat with the next family.

Many people who decide not to adopt their first foster-to-adopt cat go on to try again with a different cat and find their perfect match on the second or third attempt. The process is designed to be flexible and judgment-free.

Getting Started

If you are interested in foster-to-adopt through Cat's Meow Cat Rescue, start by browsing our available cats online or visiting us at one of our PetSmart adoption events in the Milwaukie and Gladstone area. Let us know you are interested in the foster-to-adopt option, and we will work with you to find a cat whose personality and needs align with your home. The best adoptions start with the freedom to take your time, and that is exactly what foster-to-adopt provides.
