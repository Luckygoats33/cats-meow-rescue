Earthquake Preparedness for Cat Owners in Portland

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue · July 1, 2026

The Risk

The Portland metro area sits near the Cascadia Subduction Zone, which is capable of producing a magnitude 9.0 earthquake. Emergency preparedness is essential for all Portland residents, including pet owners. FEMA recommends that pet owners include their animals in all emergency planning. A disaster plan that does not include your cat is incomplete.

Emergency Kit for Your Cat

Prepare a cat emergency kit that includes: 2 weeks of food and water, medications (with copies of prescriptions), a carrier for each cat, litter and a disposable litter pan, copies of vaccination records and microchip information, a recent photo of your cat, a leash or harness, and comfort items like a familiar blanket. Store the kit near your household emergency supplies.

During an Earthquake

During an earthquake, your cat will likely hide. Do not try to catch them during the shaking. After the shaking stops, close all windows and exterior doors before your cat tries to escape. Check for injuries. If you must evacuate, put your cat in their carrier immediately. If you cannot find your cat, leave food, water, and an open carrier in a safe room and leave the house open if safe to do so.

Evacuation Planning

Know your evacuation routes and identify pet-friendly emergency shelters in advance. The Oregon Humane Society and Red Cross maintain lists of emergency pet shelters. Many Portland-area hotels accept pets during declared emergencies. If you evacuate, take your cat. Animals left behind in disasters face injury, starvation, and death.
