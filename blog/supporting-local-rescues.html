10 Ways to Support Local Animal Rescues Without Adopting

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue · July 3, 2025

You Do Not Have to Adopt to Make a Difference

Not everyone is in a position to adopt a cat. Maybe your lease does not allow pets, maybe you travel frequently, or maybe you already have a full house. That is perfectly okay. There are dozens of meaningful ways to support cat rescues without bringing a cat home. At Cat's Meow Cat Rescue, we rely on community support in many forms, and adoption is just one of them.

1. Foster a Cat Temporarily

Fostering is not the same as adopting. You provide a temporary home for a cat until they find their forever family. Cat's Meow supplies all food, litter, and medical care. You provide the love and the space. Foster stays can be as short as two weeks or as long as a couple of months, depending on the cat's needs and adoption timeline.

2. Make a Financial Donation

Monetary donations are the lifeblood of small rescues. Veterinary bills are our largest expense, with spaying, neutering, vaccinations, and emergency care adding up quickly. Even a one-time donation of $25 can cover a cat's microchip and flea treatment. As a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit, donations to Cat's Meow Cat Rescue are tax-deductible.

3. Donate Supplies

Rescue organizations always need physical supplies. Items that are consistently in demand include:

  • Cat food (wet and dry)
  • Clumping cat litter
  • Cat carriers and crates
  • Blankets and towels
  • Cleaning supplies
  • Cat toys and scratching posts

4. Volunteer Your Time

Volunteers keep rescues running. At Cat's Meow, we need help at PetSmart adoption events, with animal transport, social media content creation, photography, and administrative tasks. Even a few hours a month makes a real difference.

5. Share Adoptable Cats on Social Media

This takes thirty seconds and costs nothing, but its impact can be enormous. When you share a post about an adoptable cat, you expose that cat to an entirely new network of potential adopters. Many of our successful adoptions started with someone sharing a post with a friend.

6. Attend and Promote Adoption Events

Even if you are not adopting, your presence at a PetSmart adoption event helps. You can encourage others, help spread the word beforehand, and bring friends who might be looking for a cat. Events with more foot traffic lead to more adoptions.

7. Use Amazon Smile or Wish Lists

Many rescues maintain Amazon Wish Lists stocked with the supplies they need most. Ordering from the list is easy, the items ship directly to the rescue, and you know your contribution is something they actually need. Check our website for our current wish list link.

8. Organize a Fundraiser

You do not need to be part of the rescue to fundraise on its behalf. Host a bake sale, set up a Facebook birthday fundraiser, organize a neighborhood garage sale, or coordinate a donation drive at your workplace. Every effort counts, and we are happy to provide materials and support.

9. Provide Professional Skills

Rescues need more than animal care. If you have skills in graphic design, web development, grant writing, accounting, photography, or marketing, your expertise is incredibly valuable. Pro bono professional services save rescues thousands of dollars annually.

10. Spread the Word About Responsible Pet Ownership

Educating your community about spaying and neutering, microchipping, keeping cats indoors, and the importance of adoption over purchasing helps prevent the overpopulation crisis that creates the need for rescues in the first place. Share articles, have conversations, and lead by example.

Every Action Counts

At Cat's Meow Cat Rescue, we see every day how small acts of support add up to big results. A donated bag of cat food feeds a foster cat for a week. A shared social media post finds a cat their forever home. A few hours at an adoption event means one more cat gets seen by the right person. You do not have to adopt to be a hero for cats in the Milwaukie, Gladstone, and Portland metro area. You just have to care enough to act.
