10 Ways to Support Local Animal Rescues Without Adopting

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue · Milwaukie, Oregon

Not everyone is in a position to adopt or foster a cat, but there are many other ways to make a meaningful impact on local animal rescue.

1. Make a Financial Donation

Even small donations add up. $25 covers a cat's food for a month. $75 covers vaccinations. $150 covers a spay/neuter surgery. Cat's Meow is a 501(c)(3) — all donations are tax-deductible. Donate here.

2. Donate Supplies

Cat food (wet and dry), litter, blankets, towels, carriers, toys, and cleaning supplies are always needed. Check with the rescue for their current wish list — needs change seasonally.

3. Share on Social Media

Sharing adoptable cat posts costs nothing but can reach hundreds of potential adopters. A simple share on Facebook or Instagram has led to many of our adoptions.

4. Volunteer Your Time

Volunteer at adoption events, help with transport, or offer professional skills like photography, design, or accounting. Even a few hours a month matters.

5. Shop Rescue Merch

Our online shop sells t-shirts, hoodies, and accessories. Every purchase supports our rescue operations.

6. Use Amazon Smile

Select Cat's Meow Cat Rescue as your charity and Amazon donates 0.5% of eligible purchases. It's free and automatic.

7. Attend Fundraising Events

Participate in rescue fundraisers — trivia nights, cat yoga, silent auctions. They're fun and they fund lifesaving work.

8. Organize a Supply Drive

Collect donations at your workplace, school, church, or community group. We'll provide collection boxes and wish lists.

9. Leave a Legacy

Include Cat's Meow in your estate planning. Planned gifts ensure the rescue's future and create a lasting impact.

10. Simply Spread the Word

Tell friends and family about rescue adoption. Recommend Cat's Meow to anyone looking for a cat. Word of mouth remains our most effective marketing tool.

For more ways to help animals, the Best Friends Animal Society has comprehensive involvement guides.
