Behind the Scenes: How a Foster-Based Cat Rescue Works

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue · January 8, 2025

What Is a Foster-Based Rescue?

A foster-based rescue is an animal rescue organization that does not operate a physical shelter building. Instead, every cat in the organization's care lives in a volunteer's home until a permanent adoptive family is found. Cat's Meow Cat Rescue in Milwaukie, Oregon operates on this model, and it is one of the most effective and humane approaches to cat rescue in use today.

Without the overhead costs of a facility, lease payments, and full-time staff, foster-based rescues can direct a much higher percentage of their funds directly toward animal care, primarily veterinary expenses. At Cat's Meow, we are an entirely volunteer-run 501(c)(3) nonprofit, which means every dollar donated goes toward saving cats.

The Journey of a Rescue Cat

Here is what typically happens when a cat enters the Cat's Meow Cat Rescue system:

  1. Intake: Cats come to us from various sources, including owner surrenders, stray situations, and transfers from overcrowded shelters. Each cat is evaluated for health and temperament.
  2. Veterinary care: New arrivals receive a full veterinary workup, including spaying or neutering, vaccinations, deworming, flea treatment, and microchipping. Cats with medical issues receive whatever treatment they need.
  3. Foster placement: The cat is matched with an appropriate foster home based on the cat's needs and the foster family's experience. Kittens may need bottle feeding. Shy cats may need a quiet home for socialization. Medical cases may need a foster with medication experience.
  4. Socialization and assessment: While in foster care, the cat's personality is observed in a real home environment. Foster parents note how the cat interacts with people, other cats, dogs, and children.
  5. Listing and promotion: The cat is photographed, a bio is written, and the listing goes live on our website, Petfinder, and social media.
  6. Adoption events: Cats attend PetSmart adoption events where prospective adopters can meet them in person.
  7. Adoption and follow-up: Once an approved adopter is matched with a cat, the adoption is finalized and the cat goes to their forever home. Cat's Meow remains available for post-adoption support.

Why the Foster Model Works

The foster model has several major advantages over traditional brick-and-mortar shelters:

  • Lower stress for cats: Cats are solitary, territorial animals that often struggle in shelter environments with noise, unfamiliar smells, and close proximity to many other cats. Foster homes provide a calm, stable setting.
  • Better health outcomes: Shelter environments increase the risk of upper respiratory infections and other contagious illnesses. Cats in foster homes are healthier overall.
  • More accurate personality assessments: A cat behaving in a foster home is a cat showing its true personality. Shelter cats may appear withdrawn or aggressive simply due to stress, making it harder for adopters to gauge compatibility.
  • Scalability: A foster-based rescue can grow its capacity simply by recruiting more foster volunteers, without the enormous expense of building expansions.

How You Can Support the Model

The foster-based model is only as strong as its network of volunteers. Here are the most impactful ways to support Cat's Meow Cat Rescue and organizations like ours:

  • Become a foster parent: This is our single greatest need. We provide all supplies, food, and veterinary care. You provide a safe, loving temporary home.
  • Donate: Financial contributions cover veterinary bills, which are our largest expense by far.
  • Volunteer at adoption events: Help us staff our PetSmart events so more cats get face time with potential adopters.
  • Share our adoptable cats on social media: A single share can reach the right person at the right time.

Every part of this system, from intake to adoption, depends on people who care. If you have ever wondered what happens behind the scenes at a cat rescue, now you know, and we hope you will consider being part of it.
