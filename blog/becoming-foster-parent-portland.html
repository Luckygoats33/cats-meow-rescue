Fostering & Foster Care

Becoming a Foster Parent for Cats in Portland

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue · July 15, 2026

Why Foster?

Fostering saves lives. Every foster home that opens up allows Cat's Meow Cat Rescue to pull another cat from a dangerous situation, whether that is an overcrowded shelter, a hoarding case, or the streets. As a foster-based rescue operating in the Portland metro area, we cannot exist without volunteer foster homes. You do not need experience, a big house, or special equipment to foster.

What We Provide

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue provides everything your foster cat needs: food, litter, veterinary care, medications, and supplies like carriers and scratching posts. Your job is to provide a safe, loving home environment, socialization, and daily care. We cover all costs. You provide the love and space.

Types of Fostering

We have several fostering options to fit different lifestyles. Long-term fosters care for adult cats until they are adopted, which can be weeks to months. Kitten fosters care for kittens or litters until they are old enough for adoption (typically 8-12 weeks). Medical fosters care for cats recovering from surgery or illness. Event fosters host cats for weekend adoption events at PetSmart.

How to Get Started

Contact Cat's Meow Cat Rescue at catsmeowcatrescue@gmail.com or (503) 850-6363 to express your interest. We will walk you through our orientation process, discuss your home setup and preferences, and match you with a foster cat that fits your lifestyle. Whether you live in a studio apartment in Portland or a house in Gladstone, there is a foster cat who would thrive in your care.
