Adoption Tips

The Ultimate Cat Adoption Checklist

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue · January 28, 2025

Adopting a cat is exciting, and a little planning ensures the process goes smoothly for both you and your new feline companion. Whether this is your first cat or your fifth, this checklist from Cat's Meow Cat Rescue covers everything you need to prepare before, during, and after adoption day.

Before You Apply

Take care of these foundational steps before submitting an adoption application:

  • Check your lease or HOA rules: If you rent, confirm that your landlord allows cats and understand any pet deposit requirements. Many apartments in the Milwaukie, Gladstone, and Portland area are cat-friendly, but always verify in writing.
  • Budget for ongoing costs: Beyond the adoption fee, plan for food ($30-60/month), litter ($15-25/month), annual vet visits ($200-400), and unexpected medical expenses. Setting aside a small pet emergency fund is wise.
  • Discuss with your household: Everyone who lives in the home should be on board with the adoption. Talk about responsibilities: who feeds the cat, who cleans the litter box, who handles vet appointments.
  • Research veterinarians: Find a vet near your home before you need one. Look for feline-friendly practices that make cats comfortable.

Supplies to Buy Before Adoption Day

Have everything set up before bringing your cat home. Here is your shopping list:

  1. Litter box (one per cat plus one extra)
  2. Unscented clumping litter
  3. Litter scoop and waste bags
  4. Food and water bowls (ceramic or stainless steel are easiest to clean)
  5. Quality cat food (ask Cat's Meow what your cat has been eating)
  6. Scratching post or pad (at least one vertical and one horizontal option)
  7. Cat bed or soft blanket
  8. Interactive toys: wand toy, crinkle balls, catnip mouse
  9. Cat carrier (hard-sided is safest for car travel)
  10. Breakaway collar with ID tag
  11. Enzymatic cleaner for accidents
  12. Nail clippers designed for cats

Adoption Day

On the day you pick up your new cat from Cat's Meow Cat Rescue or our PetSmart partner location, keep these tips in mind:

  • Bring your carrier lined with a familiar-smelling towel or blanket.
  • Drive directly home. Avoid stops, loud music, or introducing the cat to friends on the way.
  • Place the carrier in the prepared safe room and open the door. Let the cat come out on their own terms.
  • Do not invite people over to meet the cat on day one. Give them quiet time to decompress.

The First Two Weeks

Use this checklist to track your cat's adjustment:

  • Cat is eating and drinking regularly (by day 2-3)
  • Cat is using the litter box consistently
  • Cat is beginning to emerge from hiding spots
  • Scheduled wellness visit with your veterinarian (within first 10-14 days)
  • Registered microchip information updated to your contact details
  • Cat has been introduced gradually to other rooms in the home
  • If applicable, slow introduction to other pets has begun

Ongoing Care Reminders

Once your cat is settled, maintain their health and happiness with these ongoing practices:

  • Annual vet checkups (twice yearly for seniors)
  • Keep vaccinations current per your vet's schedule
  • Monthly flea prevention, especially important in Oregon's damp climate
  • Daily play sessions to keep your cat physically and mentally active
  • Regular nail trims every 2-3 weeks
  • Clean litter boxes daily and replace litter completely every 1-2 weeks

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue is here for you even after adoption day. If you run into challenges or have questions, reach out to our team. A prepared adopter is a successful adopter, and a successful adoption means one more cat living the life they deserve.
