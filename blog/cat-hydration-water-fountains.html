Cat Hydration: Why Water Fountains Are Worth It

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue · July 8, 2026

Why Cats Don't Drink Enough

Domestic cats evolved from desert-dwelling ancestors who obtained most of their moisture from prey. This evolutionary legacy means modern cats have a low thirst drive and often do not drink enough water from a bowl. Chronic mild dehydration is common in cats and contributes to urinary tract problems, kidney disease, and constipation, all of which are among the most common feline health issues.

The Benefits of Cat Water Fountains

Cat water fountains provide a continuous flow of filtered, oxygenated water that many cats find more appealing than still water in a bowl. Studies have shown that cats with access to a fountain drink significantly more water than those drinking from a bowl. This increased water intake helps flush the urinary system, support kidney function, and maintain overall hydration.

Choosing the Right Fountain

Look for a fountain made of ceramic or stainless steel rather than plastic, which can harbor bacteria and cause chin acne in some cats. The fountain should have a replaceable filter, be easy to disassemble for cleaning, and have a quiet pump. Popular options include the Catit Flower Fountain, PetSafe Drinkwell, and ceramic fountains from Miaustore.

Other Hydration Strategies

In addition to a water fountain, you can increase your cat's water intake by feeding wet food (which is 70-80% water), adding a small amount of water to dry food, placing multiple water sources around your home, and keeping water dishes away from food bowls and litter boxes. Fresh, clean water should always be available.
