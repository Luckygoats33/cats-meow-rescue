Preparing Your Foster Cat for Adoption Day

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue · July 2, 2026

The Pre-Adoption Checklist

Before a foster cat goes to their new home, ensure they are current on vaccinations, spayed or neutered, microchipped, and free of parasites. Cat's Meow Cat Rescue coordinates all veterinary care, but foster parents should confirm that all medical steps are complete. Document the cat's current food, litter preference, and daily routine.

Writing the Cat's Bio

Foster parents know the cat better than anyone. Write a detailed description of the cat's personality, quirks, favorite activities, and any special needs. Does the cat love being held? Do they knead on fleece blankets? Are they a window watcher or a lap cat? These details help potential adopters fall in love and make informed decisions.

Adoption Day Tips

On adoption day, pack a small bag with the cat's current food, a familiar blanket or toy, and any medications. Brief the adopter on the cat's routine, preferences, and any behavioral notes. A smooth handoff sets the stage for a successful transition. It is emotional to say goodbye, but knowing you prepared them well makes it easier.

After They Leave

It is completely normal to feel sad after your foster cat is adopted. Give yourself time to process, then remember that your open home means another cat can be saved. Cat's Meow Cat Rescue will let you know how your former foster is settling in, and many adopters share updates and photos with foster families.
