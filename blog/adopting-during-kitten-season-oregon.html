Adopting During Kitten Season in Oregon

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue · July 6, 2026

What Is Kitten Season?

Kitten season typically runs from April through October in Oregon, peaking in late spring and summer. During this time, unspayed outdoor and feral cats give birth, and shelters and rescues are flooded with kittens. Cat's Meow Cat Rescue takes in dozens of kittens during this period, and our foster homes are stretched to capacity.

Why Adoption Matters Most During Kitten Season

During kitten season, rescues need adopters more than ever. Every kitten that gets adopted opens a foster spot for the next litter that comes in. At Cat's Meow, we cannot say yes to incoming kittens if all of our foster homes are full. Your adoption directly saves multiple lives by creating space in our system.

Beyond Kittens: Adult Cats Need Homes Too

Ironically, kitten season is the hardest time for adult cats to get adopted. Everyone wants a kitten, and adult cats get overlooked. If you are looking for a calm, already-trained companion, kitten season is an excellent time to adopt an adult cat from Cat's Meow Cat Rescue. You might find the perfect match without any competition.

How to Help Even If You Cannot Adopt

Foster a litter of kittens, donate supplies like kitten formula and heating pads, volunteer at our PetSmart adoption events in Clackamas and Portland, or simply share our available cats on social media. Every action makes a difference during the busiest time of year for cat rescues in Oregon.
