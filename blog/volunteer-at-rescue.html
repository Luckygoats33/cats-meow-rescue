How to Volunteer at a Cat Rescue in Oregon

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue · January 22, 2025

Why Volunteers Are the Heart of Every Rescue

Cat rescue organizations across Oregon rely almost entirely on volunteers to carry out their missions. At Cat's Meow Cat Rescue in Milwaukie, every single person involved donates their time. We have no paid staff. From the people who foster kittens in their homes to the volunteers who staff adoption events at PetSmart, our work would not be possible without community members stepping up.

Volunteering at a cat rescue is also deeply rewarding on a personal level. Studies consistently show that spending time with animals reduces stress and improves mood, and there is something uniquely satisfying about knowing you played a direct role in helping a cat find a safe, loving home.

Types of Volunteer Roles Available

One of the best things about volunteering with a rescue is the variety of roles available. You do not need to be a veterinarian or even have previous animal experience. Here are some of the most common volunteer positions:

  • Foster parent: Provide temporary housing and care for cats and kittens until they are adopted. This is our greatest ongoing need.
  • Adoption event volunteer: Help set up, manage cat enclosures, greet visitors, and facilitate meet-and-greets at PetSmart events.
  • Transport driver: Pick up cats from shelters, drive them to veterinary appointments, or deliver them to foster homes.
  • Social media and photography: Take quality photos and write compelling bios for adoptable cats to share online.
  • Administrative support: Help with data entry, email correspondence, donation tracking, and event coordination.
  • Fundraising and outreach: Organize donation drives, coordinate with local businesses, and represent the rescue at community events.

What to Expect When You Apply

Most Oregon cat rescues, including Cat's Meow, have a simple volunteer application process. Here is what a typical path looks like:

  1. Fill out a volunteer application on our website or at an adoption event.
  2. Brief orientation: Learn about our mission, protocols, and the specific role you are interested in.
  3. Get matched to a role based on your skills, availability, and interests.
  4. Start helping: Most volunteers begin contributing within their first week.

We are flexible with scheduling. Whether you can commit to a few hours a month or several hours a week, there is a role that fits your availability.

Skills That Rescues Need Most

While anyone with a willing heart can volunteer, certain skills are especially valuable to rescue organizations:

  • Photography and graphic design for creating adoption listings and social media content.
  • Grant writing and nonprofit experience to help secure funding.
  • Veterinary or vet tech experience for assessing incoming cats and administering basic care.
  • Event planning for organizing fundraisers and community outreach.
  • Web development or IT skills for maintaining the rescue's online presence.

How to Get Started in the Portland Metro Area

If you are in the Milwaukie, Gladstone, or greater Portland area and want to volunteer with Cat's Meow Cat Rescue, visit our website to fill out a volunteer interest form. You can also stop by one of our PetSmart adoption events to meet current volunteers and learn more about what we do.

Other Oregon rescues that welcome volunteers include the Oregon Humane Society, Cat Adoption Team, and Furry Friends. No matter which organization you choose, your time will make a real difference in the lives of cats who need help the most.
