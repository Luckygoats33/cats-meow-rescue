Pet Food Banks in Portland: Keeping Families Together

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue · July 12, 2026

Why Pet Food Banks Matter

Financial hardship is one of the leading reasons cats are surrendered to rescues. Pet food banks prevent this by providing free food and supplies to pet owners who are struggling. Keeping a cat with its family is always better than the cat entering the rescue system, both for the cat's wellbeing and for the emotional health of the owner.

Portland-Area Resources

The Oregon Food Bank includes pet food in some distributions. PAW Team provides free veterinary care and pet food to pets of people experiencing homelessness. The Pixie Project offers a pet food pantry. Many churches and community organizations in Milwaukie, Gladstone, and the greater Portland area also collect and distribute pet food.

How Cat's Meow Helps

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue believes in keeping families together. If you are struggling to afford food for your cat, contact us. We can connect you with pet food resources, provide temporary supplies, and help you find solutions that allow you to keep your cat. Surrendering your cat to a rescue should always be a last resort, and we will help you explore every alternative.

Donating to Pet Food Banks

If you are in a position to help, most pet food banks accept donations of unopened cat food (both wet and dry), cat litter, and basic supplies. Drop-off locations are available throughout the Portland metro area. Even a single bag of cat food can make the difference between a family keeping their cat and having to give them up.
