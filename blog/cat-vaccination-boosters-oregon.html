Cat Vaccination Boosters: When and Why They Matter

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue · June 29, 2026

Why Boosters Are Necessary

Initial vaccinations prime your cat's immune system, but protection wanes over time. Booster shots are follow-up doses that reinforce immunity and ensure lasting protection against diseases like feline distemper, calicivirus, and rabies. Without timely boosters, your cat may become vulnerable to preventable diseases.

The Typical Booster Schedule

Kittens receive a series of vaccinations every 3-4 weeks from 6-16 weeks of age. Adult cats typically need boosters one year after their initial series, then every 1-3 years depending on the vaccine. Rabies boosters are required by Oregon law. Your Portland-area veterinarian will recommend a schedule based on your cat's risk factors and lifestyle.

What's Included in Cat's Meow Adoptions

Indoor Cats Still Need Vaccines

Even indoor cats need core vaccinations. Viruses can be carried into the home on shoes and clothing. Cats may escape unexpectedly. Boarding facilities and veterinary clinics require vaccination records. Indoor cats at minimum need FVRCP and rabies vaccinations kept current.
