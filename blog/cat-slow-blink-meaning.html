The Cat Slow Blink: What It Means and How to Do It

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue · July 13, 2026

The Feline Kiss

When a cat looks at you and slowly closes their eyes, then opens them again, they are communicating trust and affection. This behavior, known as the slow blink or cat kiss, means the cat feels safe enough in your presence to close their eyes, which would leave them vulnerable in the wild. It is one of the highest compliments a cat can pay you.

Scientific Validation

Research published in the journal Scientific Reports confirmed that cats respond positively to human slow blinks. In the study, cats were more likely to slow blink back at their owners who slow blinked at them, and they were more likely to approach an unfamiliar person who slow blinked compared to one who maintained a neutral expression.

How to Slow Blink at Your Cat

Look at your cat with soft, relaxed eyes. Slowly close your eyes halfway, pause, then close them fully for a beat before slowly opening them again. Do not stare intensely before or after. The key is soft, relaxed eye contact followed by a deliberate, slow closure. Many cats will slow blink back, and over time, this becomes a meaningful communication ritual between you.

Building Trust with Shy Cats

At Cat's Meow Cat Rescue, we teach foster parents to use slow blinking with shy or newly arrived cats. It is a non-threatening way to communicate safety and affection without requiring physical contact. For cats coming from traumatic backgrounds, slow blinking can be the first step toward building trust with humans.
