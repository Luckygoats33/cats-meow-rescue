Cat's Meow Cat Rescue · July 1, 2026

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue: Your Neighborhood Rescue

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue is based right here in Milwaukie, Oregon, serving the Milwaukie, Gladstone, and greater Clackamas County community. As a foster-based 501(c)(3) nonprofit, we rescue cats from high-risk situations and place them in loving foster homes until they find their forever families. Our PO Box is located at PO Box 68222, Milwaukie, OR 97268.

Local Adoption Events

We hold regular adoption events at PetSmart locations in the Clackamas area where you can meet our available cats in person. These events are typically held on weekends and are a great way to interact with cats and kittens in a low-pressure setting. Follow us on Facebook for event schedules and featured cats.

Other Local Resources

The Milwaukie and Gladstone area has several pet-related resources including Milwaukie Veterinary Hospital, Clackamas Pet Clinic, and Gladstone Animal Hospital for veterinary care. Pet supply stores like Wilco Farm Stores and local pet boutiques offer quality food and supplies. The Clackamas County Dog Services also handles stray cat intake and can direct you to local rescues.

How to Get Started

Visit our website to browse available cats, submit an adoption application, or contact us at catsmeowcatrescue@gmail.com or (503) 850-6363. We are happy to answer any questions about the adoption process and help you find your perfect feline companion right here in your neighborhood.
