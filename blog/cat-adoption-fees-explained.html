Cat Adoption Fees Explained: What You're Actually Paying For

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue · July 14, 2026

What's Included in the Adoption Fee

At Cat's Meow Cat Rescue, our adoption fee covers spay or neuter surgery, age-appropriate vaccinations (FVRCP and rabies), deworming, flea treatment, microchipping with registration, FIV/FeLV testing, and a veterinary health examination. If you purchased these services individually from a veterinarian in the Portland area, you would easily spend $400-$800 or more. Our adoption fees are significantly less than that because we work with partner veterinary clinics who support our mission.

Why Rescues Charge Adoption Fees

Adoption fees help us continue our life-saving work. As a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, Cat's Meow Cat Rescue relies entirely on adoption fees, donations, and fundraising to cover the cost of care for our cats. Many of the cats we take in need additional medical treatment beyond the basics, such as dental work, treatment for upper respiratory infections, or surgery for injuries. The adoption fee from one cat helps fund the care of the next cat in need.

Comparing Costs: Adoption vs. Breeder vs. Pet Store

A purebred kitten from a breeder can cost $500-$2,000 or more, often without any veterinary care included. Pet store kittens, frequently sourced from kitten mills, can cost $300-$1,500 with minimal health guarantees. An adopted cat from Cat's Meow comes fully vetted, behaviorally assessed, and with ongoing support from our team. From a financial perspective alone, adoption is the best value available.

Fee Waivers and Specials

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue occasionally offers reduced adoption fees for senior cats, bonded pairs, or cats that have been in our care for an extended period. We also participate in special adoption events at PetSmart locations in the Clackamas and Portland area where promotional pricing may apply. Follow us on Facebook for announcements about upcoming specials.
