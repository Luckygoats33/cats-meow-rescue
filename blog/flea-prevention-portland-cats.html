Year-Round Flea Prevention for Portland Cats

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue · July 2, 2026

Why Portland Cats Need Year-Round Protection

Unlike regions with harsh winters that kill flea populations, Portland's mild, damp climate allows fleas to survive year-round. Fleas can thrive in Oregon's temperatures even in winter, especially in sheltered environments like garages, crawl spaces, and under porches. Indoor cats can still get fleas from fleas that hitch a ride on your clothing or from shared walls in apartments.

Choosing the Right Prevention

Monthly topical treatments (like Revolution Plus or Advantage II), oral medications (like Comfortis), and long-lasting options (like Bravecto, which lasts 3 months) are all effective. Avoid over-the-counter flea products from grocery stores, which can be ineffective or even dangerous. Never use dog flea products on cats, as permethrin-based dog treatments are toxic and potentially fatal to cats.

Treating an Active Infestation

If your cat already has fleas, treat all pets in the household simultaneously. Wash all bedding in hot water, vacuum thoroughly (including furniture and under cushions), and dispose of the vacuum bag or empty the canister outside. Treat the environment with a vet-recommended household spray. The entire cycle takes 3-4 months to fully resolve because flea pupae in the environment are resistant to treatment.

Natural Supplementation

While natural flea remedies alone are generally not sufficient in Portland's climate, they can supplement conventional prevention. Food-grade diatomaceous earth can be sprinkled on bedding and vacuumed up after 24 hours. A flea comb used daily can catch fleas early. However, for reliable protection, veterinary-recommended preventive medication is essential.
