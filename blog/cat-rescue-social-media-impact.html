How Social Media Saves Cats: The Power of Sharing

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue · July 2, 2026

The Algorithm Helps Animals

Social media algorithms prioritize content that generates engagement. When you like, comment on, or share a post about an adoptable cat, you increase its visibility to hundreds or thousands of additional people. A single viral post about a cat can generate dozens of adoption applications. Your engagement literally saves lives.

What to Share

The most impactful shares are individual cat profiles with clear photos and engaging descriptions. Posts about urgent cases, special needs cats, and cats who have been waiting longest benefit most from additional visibility. Cat's Meow Cat Rescue regularly posts available cats on Facebook. Sharing these posts to your personal timeline or local community groups is one of the simplest ways to help.

Photography Matters

A well-photographed cat receives dramatically more interest than a poorly photographed one. Natural lighting, clean backgrounds, and photos showing the cat's personality (playing, purring, making eye contact) generate the most engagement. If you are a photographer, offering to take professional photos of adoptable cats is one of the highest-impact volunteer activities.

Beyond Facebook

While Facebook remains our primary platform, sharing on Instagram, Nextdoor, and local Reddit communities also helps. The Portland subreddit and neighborhood-specific Nextdoor groups are excellent places to share available cats. Every platform reaches a different audience, and your cat's perfect adopter might be on a platform we have not yet reached.
