Corporate Sponsorship for Cat Rescues: How Businesses Can Help

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue · June 29, 2026

Why Sponsor a Cat Rescue?

Corporate sponsorship of cat rescues provides businesses with community goodwill, positive PR, employee engagement opportunities, and tax-deductible charitable giving. For rescues like Cat's Meow Cat Rescue, corporate sponsors provide stable funding that enables us to plan and grow our life-saving programs.

Sponsorship Options

Businesses can sponsor Cat's Meow Cat Rescue in many ways: event sponsorship, monthly giving programs, in-kind donations of products or services, employee matching gift programs, and cause marketing partnerships. We are flexible and creative in structuring partnerships that benefit both the business and our mission.

Employee Engagement

Many Portland companies offer volunteer days and team-building events centered around charitable work. Cat's Meow Cat Rescue can host team volunteer events, provide guest speakers for company wellness programs, and create customized engagement opportunities that align with your corporate social responsibility goals.

Getting Started

Contact Cat's Meow Cat Rescue at catsmeowcatrescue@gmail.com to discuss sponsorship opportunities. We will work with you to create a partnership that aligns with your business goals and makes a meaningful impact on the lives of cats in the Portland metro area.
