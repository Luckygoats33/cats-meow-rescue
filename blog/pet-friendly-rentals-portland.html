Finding Pet-Friendly Rentals in Portland with a Cat

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue · July 11, 2026

Oregon Rental Laws and Pets

Oregon does not have a statewide law requiring landlords to allow pets. However, Portland's competitive rental market means many landlords do accept cats, especially since cats typically cause less damage than dogs. Under Oregon law, landlords can charge a refundable pet deposit of up to the equivalent of one month's rent. They cannot charge non-refundable pet fees, which is good news for renters with cats.

Where to Find Cat-Friendly Rentals

Neighborhoods like Milwaukie, Gladstone, Sellwood, and Foster-Powell tend to have a good selection of cat-friendly rentals at more affordable prices than inner Portland. Websites like Zillow, Apartments.com, and Craigslist allow you to filter for pet-friendly units. Local Facebook groups like Portland Rentals and SE Portland Housing also frequently list cat-friendly options.

Making Your Application Stand Out

When applying for a rental with a cat, include a pet resume with your application. This should include your cat's name, age, weight, vaccination records, spay/neuter status, and a brief description of their temperament. If your cat was adopted from Cat's Meow Cat Rescue, mention that they are fully vetted and microchipped. A letter from a previous landlord confirming no pet damage can also help.

Tips for Cat Owners in Apartments

Keep your cat indoors, provide vertical space with cat trees and shelves, maintain a clean litter box to prevent odors, and protect window screens. These simple steps make apartment living with a cat easy and enjoyable for both you and your neighbors. A well-cared-for indoor cat in a Portland apartment can live 15-20 years.
