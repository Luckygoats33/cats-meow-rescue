Your New Cat's First Vet Visit: What to Expect

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue · Milwaukie, Oregon

Every Cat's Meow cat comes fully vetted before adoption, but scheduling a wellness check with your own veterinarian within the first week is an important step. Here's what to expect and how to prepare.

What We've Already Done

All cats adopted from Cat's Meow Cat Rescue receive: spay/neuter surgery, FVRCP and rabies vaccinations, microchipping, deworming, flea treatment, and FIV/FeLV testing. You'll receive complete medical records at adoption. Your vet visit is about establishing a baseline and building a relationship with a local veterinarian.

Choosing a Vet

If you don't already have a vet, ask for recommendations from friends, neighbors, or your Cat's Meow foster parent. For Milwaukie and Gladstone residents, there are several excellent clinics in the area. The Oregon Veterinary Medical Association maintains a searchable directory.

What the Vet Will Check

Expect a thorough physical exam: weight, temperature, heart and lung sounds, dental check, ear and eye examination, and palpation of the abdomen. Your vet will review the medical records we provide and discuss a preventive care schedule going forward. They may recommend additional tests based on your cat's age and health history.

Questions to Ask

Come prepared with questions: What food do you recommend? What's the vaccination schedule going forward? How often should I bring my cat in for checkups? Are there any concerns based on the exam? What emergency clinic do you recommend for after-hours care?

Transporting Your Cat

Use a hard-sided carrier and line it with a familiar blanket or towel. Cover the carrier with a light cloth during the car ride — it reduces visual stimulation and helps keep your cat calm. Drive gently and keep the radio low.
