What Your Cat's Sleeping Position Says About Them

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue · June 30, 2026

The Loaf

When a cat tucks all four paws underneath their body and sits upright with eyes partially closed, they are in the classic loaf position. This indicates a cat that is relaxed but alert. They can spring into action quickly if needed. It is a common resting position for cats who feel safe but are still monitoring their environment.

Belly Up

A cat sleeping completely on their back with their belly exposed is displaying the ultimate sign of trust and security. The belly is a vulnerable area, and exposing it means the cat feels absolutely safe in their environment. However, an exposed belly is not always an invitation to pet it. Many cats will reflexively grab and kick if their belly is touched.

The Curled Ball

Curling into a tight ball conserves body heat and protects vital organs. Cats who sleep curled up may be seeking warmth or may be in a new environment where they feel they need to protect themselves. It is a very common position for newly adopted cats during the adjustment period.

The Superman

When a cat stretches out flat on their stomach with legs extended front and back, they are fully relaxed and also positioned to cool down. This position maximizes contact with cool surfaces. Portland cat owners often see this during summer heat waves when cats stretch out on tile or hardwood floors.
