Summer Cat Care in Portland: Heat Safety and Fun

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue · July 8, 2026

Heat Safety

Portland summers have become increasingly hot, with heat waves regularly exceeding 100 degrees. Cats can overheat quickly. Signs of heat stress include panting, drooling, lethargy, and vomiting. Keep your home cool with air conditioning or fans, provide multiple water sources, and ensure your cat has access to cool, shaded areas. Never leave a cat in a car, even briefly.

Hydration

Increase water availability during hot weather. Place extra water bowls around your home, add ice cubes to water, and consider a cat water fountain. Wet food provides additional moisture. Some cats enjoy licking ice cubes or frozen broth treats. Monitor your cat's water intake and watch for signs of dehydration (sunken eyes, dry gums, lethargy).

Outdoor Safety

If your cat has access to a catio or enclosed outdoor space, ensure it has shade and ventilation. Avoid letting cats on catios during the hottest part of the day (11 AM - 4 PM) during heat waves. Check concrete and metal surfaces, which can burn paw pads. Portland's wildfire smoke season (typically August-September) is another reason to keep cats indoors.

Summer Fun

Summer in Portland also means more daylight for play. Take advantage of long evenings for interactive play sessions. Freeze treats inside ice cubes for enrichment. Set up a bird feeder outside a window for entertainment. Cat grass and catnip plants thrive in Portland summers and provide safe indoor greenery for your cat to enjoy.
