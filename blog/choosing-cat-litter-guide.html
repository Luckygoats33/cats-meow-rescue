Choosing the Right Cat Litter: A Complete Guide

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue · July 15, 2026

Types of Cat Litter

The main types of cat litter include clay clumping (the most popular), clay non-clumping, silica crystal, natural/biodegradable (pine, corn, wheat, walnut), and recycled paper. Each has pros and cons related to odor control, dust levels, tracking, environmental impact, and cost. Most cats adapt well to different litter types, though sudden changes can cause litter box avoidance.

Clumping Clay: The Standard

Clumping clay litter, usually made from sodium bentonite, is the most popular choice. It forms solid clumps when wet, making daily scooping easy and extending the life of the litter. It has excellent odor control and is widely available at all price points. Downsides include dust production and the fact that it is not biodegradable.

Natural and Eco-Friendly Options

Portland cat owners interested in sustainability have excellent natural litter options. Pine pellet litter (like Feline Pine) is made from lumber industry byproducts. Corn-based litters (World's Best Cat Litter) are flushable and biodegradable. Walnut shell litter (Naturally Fresh) offers excellent clumping. These options are better for the environment and often produce less dust than clay.

What Most Cats Prefer

Research shows that most cats prefer fine-grained, unscented clumping litter. Avoid heavily scented litters, which can be overwhelming to a cat's sensitive nose and may cause litter box avoidance. When switching litter types, transition gradually by mixing the new litter with the old over 7-10 days. At Cat's Meow Cat Rescue, we inform adopters about each cat's current litter preference.
