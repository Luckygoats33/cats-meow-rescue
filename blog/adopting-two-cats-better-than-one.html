Why Adopting Two Cats Is Often Better Than One

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue · June 29, 2026

Companionship When You Are Away

If you work outside the home or travel frequently, a single cat can become bored and lonely. Two cats keep each other company, play together, and provide social stimulation that prevents destructive behavior. At Cat's Meow Cat Rescue, we frequently have bonded pairs available who are already best friends and are looking for a home together.

Better Adjustment for Kittens

Kittens especially benefit from having a companion. A single kitten can develop behavioral issues from pent-up energy, including biting, excessive meowing, and destructive play. Two kittens tire each other out, learn social boundaries from each other, and are generally better-behaved than solo kittens. Many rescues, including Cat's Meow, offer discounted adoption fees for pairs.

The Math Works Out

The incremental cost of a second cat is lower than you might think. You need one additional litter box, slightly more food, and one more annual vet visit. The love, entertainment, and companionship you receive in return is immeasurable. Two cats curled up together on your couch is one of the most heartwarming sights a pet owner can experience.
