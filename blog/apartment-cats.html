Best Cat Breeds and Types for Apartment Living in Portland

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue · Milwaukie, Oregon

Living in a Portland apartment doesn't mean you can't have a cat — far from it. Many cats thrive in smaller spaces, especially when given proper enrichment. Here's what to look for when adopting a cat for apartment life.

Temperament Over Breed

While certain breeds are known for being adaptable to small spaces (British Shorthairs, Ragdolls, Russian Blues), temperament matters more than breed for apartment living. At Cat's Meow, we can match you with cats our foster parents have identified as calm, quiet, and content in smaller spaces.

Adult Cats Are Your Best Bet

Kittens have boundless energy and need room to run. An adult cat (2-7 years) or a mellow senior cat is often a better fit for apartment living. Their energy levels are predictable, and they've outgrown the need to climb every surface.

Essential Apartment Cat Setup

Maximize vertical space with cat trees, window perches, and wall-mounted shelves. Cats feel secure when they can survey their territory from above. Place the litter box in a private but accessible spot — bathrooms and closets work well. And invest in enrichment toys and puzzle feeders to keep your indoor cat mentally stimulated.

Managing Common Apartment Concerns

Worried about noise? Most cats are quiet — especially adults. Concerned about smell? A covered litter box with quality litter, scooped daily, won't bother your neighbors. Deposits? Many Portland landlords waive pet deposits for cats, which cause far less damage than dogs.

Indoor-Only Benefits

Apartment cats are indoor-only by default, which is actually safer. Indoor cats live significantly longer than outdoor cats. They're protected from traffic, predators, disease, and the unpredictable Oregon weather.

Ready to find your apartment-friendly companion? Apply to adopt and mention your living situation — we'll match you perfectly. The Cat Adoption Team in Sherwood is another excellent local resource for finding apartment-friendly cats.
