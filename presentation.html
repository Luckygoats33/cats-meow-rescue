❓ Feline Curious? Common Questions

  • "Will our URL change?" — Nope! catsmeowcatrescue.com stays the same. 😸
  • "Will we lose Google rankings?" — No. Proper redirects preserve SEO. The new site scores 97 vs. 58 — rankings should improve.
  • "How long is the site down?" — Zero downtime. Both sites work during DNS switch.
  • "Is it secure for payments?" — Yes. Stripe handles all payment data (PCI-DSS Level 1). No card numbers on our server.
  • "What if the developer leaves?" — WordPress is the world's most popular CMS. Any developer can maintain it. 🔓
  • "How hard is it to update?" — Like using Microsoft Word. Blog post = 10 min. Cat status update = 2 min. 🐾
