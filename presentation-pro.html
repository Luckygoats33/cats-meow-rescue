Frequently Asked Questions

  • "Will our URL change?" — No. catsmeowcatrescue.com stays the same.
  • "Will we lose Google rankings?" — No. Proper redirects preserve SEO. The new site scores 97 vs. 58 — rankings should improve.
  • "How long is the site down?" — Zero downtime. Both sites work during DNS switch (1–24 hours).
  • "Is it secure for payments?" — Yes. Stripe handles all payment data (PCI-DSS Level 1 certified). No card numbers touch our server.
  • "What if the developer leaves?" — WordPress is the world's most popular CMS. Any developer can maintain it.
  • "How hard is it to update?" — Similar to using Microsoft Word. Blog post takes ~10 minutes. Cat status update takes ~2 minutes.
