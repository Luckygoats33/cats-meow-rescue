How to play

  • Aim: drag back from the sling and let go. On a phone, drag on the picture. A short pull is a soft toss.
  • Laser eyes: after a launch, while that cat is still in play, tap the yard where the beam should go, press Laser eyes, or press Space. Arrows aim the beam. Each cat gets one short shot. It cuts cardboard, vacuums, and pests. Wood and stone need a solid hit.
  • Clear the yard: knock out every vacuum bot, stone dog statue, and cardboard pest. Leftover boards are fine. Four yards, a few cats each.
  • Stars: three if the first cat clears the yard, two if you still have a cat left, one if the last cat does it.
  • Restart replays the yard you are on. On the last card, Play again starts from the porch. Sound is a few soft beeps, and you can turn it off.

Cat's Meow Cat Rescue is a foster-based 501(c)(3) in Milwaukie and the Portland metro. This game is only for fun — it does not take applications or gifts. When you want the real thing: apply to adopt, see how to adopt, foster, or donate.
